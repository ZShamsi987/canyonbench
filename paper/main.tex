\begin{abstract}
We introduce CanyonBench-Trace, a procedural benchmark that uses a calibrated
virtual camera and exact projected map masks to measure feature acuity,
localization, and causal use of visual evidence for water, major roads, and
cultivated fields. The design contains 120 independent sites, 960 clean views,
and a singly degraded subset spanning 3--24\,km AGL and two view geometries.
Target, matched-distractor, model-self-evidence, and control interventions
separate oracle reliance from the faithfulness of a model's claimed evidence.
