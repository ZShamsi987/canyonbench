The base unit is a geographic site. We use 60 flight-corridor, 36 regional-OOD,
and 24 cross-biome sites, balanced across water, major roads, and cultivated
fields and within-class presence. Imagery normally comes from NAIP; independent
hydrography, road, and crop layers establish consensus labels. Optional 3DEP
terrain supports depth-aware matching.

Four automatic gates enforce source-date alignment, cross-source consensus,
resolvability or explicit extinction status, and exclusion-only detector
screening. Human involvement is limited to two independent objective auditors
on a stratified 5--10\% sample.

Each site is rendered at 3, 8, 16, and 24\,km AGL in nadir and moderate-oblique
geometry at 1024 pixels and 40-degree horizontal field of view. This yields 960
clean views. A stratified 240-view subset receives exactly one degradation
calibrated from real balloon video. RGB, all masks, and depth use one identical
source quadrilateral and homography.

A view enters the extinction band only when apparent width is below the
geometric threshold, local contrast against surrounding texture is below the
calibrated bound, the independent exclusion detector reports no evidence, and
the human audit sample confirms no visible trace. Extinction cases are retained
and flagged, kept separate from ordinary negatives that contain no feature and
from ordinary positives that contain a resolvable one.

Because the source is already orthorectified, a synthesized nadir view omits the
relief displacement a real camera would record. That displacement is
$d = r\,\Delta h / H$ for radial distance $r$, within-view relief $\Delta h$, and
flying height $H$; it corrupts no label, since image and masks share one
transform. We quantify it per view from the projected terrain and report it as a
sim-to-real fidelity gap rather than concealing it. \TBD{Report the frozen median
and maximum relief displacement in pixels per altitude.}
