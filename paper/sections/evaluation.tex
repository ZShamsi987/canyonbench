\section{Causal Evidence Tracing}
Models return a constrained answer, confidence, up to $K$ evidence cells, and a
ranking of exactly those cells. Malformed output is retried at most three times
and then reported as format failure.

For oracle deletion, the most locally resolved parts of the exact mask are
suppressed at 0/25/50/75/100\%. Matched-distractor deletion repeats the operator
on an irrelevant region balanced on area, texture, edges, brightness, center
distance, boundary complexity, and depth. Self-deletion removes ranked claimed
cells one at a time; sufficiency preserves them while suppressing the rest.
Random and texture-matched cells are controls. Strong local blur, background
texture replacement, and frequency removal are all primary; inpainting is
secondary pending artifact audit.
All curves are summarized by area over the perturbation curve, following the
comprehensiveness/sufficiency convention in ERASER \cite{deyoung2020eraser}.
Operator-specific values and rank agreement are retained rather than selecting
the most favorable operator \cite{basu2026ice}.

Tier A screens all 960 views. Tier B traces a stratified 160. Tier C evaluates
prompts and CAVE on 120. We report accuracy/localization, OCRS, SEN, SES, EFS,
OSG, false-premise prompt-prior gap, extinction positive rate, psychometric
acuity, and risk--coverage curves.

\section{Validation and Statistics}
V1--V6 test synthetic feature presentation, edit detectability, operator
agreement, grid/$K$ sensitivity, reference baselines, and suppression efficacy.
Intervals bootstrap geographic groups and sites. Comparisons are paired by
site. Registered mixed effects include scale, apparent width, area, geometry,
quality, prompt, intervention, and model class. Benjamini--Hochberg correction
covers macro FPR, OCRS, and EFS.

\section{CAVE}
CAVE re-queries a positive under claimed-cell deletion, claimed-cell
preservation, and matched nuisance deletion. It accepts only if development-set
thresholds on confidence, necessity, sufficiency, and nuisance all pass;
otherwise it abstains. We report reliability, coverage, and cost frontiers plus
component ablations.
