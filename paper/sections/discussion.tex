\section{Discussion and Limitations}
The benchmark isolates visible-image faithfulness more carefully than point-sampled geographic labels, but remains limited by one flight, correlated terrain coverage, planar registration over canyon relief, RGB ambiguity, source vintage differences, and human uncertainty. Registration failure narrows the grounding population rather than being hidden. The benchmark evaluates perception and evidence localization, not safe autonomous action.
