\section{Introduction}
Vision-language models are increasingly considered for rapid terrain summarization when a full remote-sensing pipeline is unavailable. In such settings, a hallucinated road, water body, or vegetation belt can misdirect attention. CanyonBench probes these failures in imagery from a high-altitude balloon trajectory over a sparse, visually distinctive canyon landscape containing both genuine positive and negative feature cases.

Our claims are limited to: (1) a human-labeled benchmark with validated registration where feasible; (2) associations between faithfulness and the ascent trajectory or effective ground sampling distance after quality and prevalence controls; and (3) susceptibility to false-premise prompts plus a single evidence-first baseline.
