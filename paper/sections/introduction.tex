\section{Introduction}
An aerial platform running a vision--language model is a deployment setting in
which grounding failures are consequential and hard to notice. A road, water
body, or cultivated field can be geographically plausible yet absent from the
scene, or present but below the image's resolvable scale. In both cases a model
can answer confidently, cite a region, and be believed. Ordinary accuracy cannot
show whether a correct answer used visible evidence, and an overlapping
localization can remain causally decorative: the cited region may overlap the
true feature while contributing nothing to the decision.

Separating these cases requires intervention rather than observation. If an
answer depends on the evidence it cites, removing that evidence should change
it; if the answer is driven by a language prior or by scene context, it will
survive. Making that test trustworthy requires three things that annotated
image sets cannot supply at once: masks exact enough that an intervention hits
the feature and nothing else, an irrelevant region matched closely enough that
generic edit sensitivity is controlled for, and a ground truth for
\emph{unresolvability} that does not depend on annotator judgment.

CanyonBench-Trace obtains all three from procedural generation. A deterministic
virtual camera over frozen orthoimagery applies one transform to RGB, feature
masks, and terrain, so masks are pixel-exact by construction with no
registration and no boundary drawing. Because the camera fixes ground sample
distance, the projected apparent width of every mapped feature is known in
pixels, which yields the benchmark's sharpest instrument: a road that is mapped
and confirmed at 3\,km becomes physically unresolvable at float altitude in the
same scene, so a positive answer there is ungrounded by imaging geometry rather
than by opinion. Water and fields remain resolvable throughout, controlling for
a model that merely becomes more conservative as altitude increases.

The protocol then intervenes on the true feature, on a covariate-matched
irrelevant region, on the cells the model itself verbalizes, and on random or
texture-matched controls. It needs only repeated queries and a structured
answer, so proprietary and open-weight systems are measured identically, and the
same measurement extends to a mitigation: CAVE re-queries a positive under
deletion, preservation, and nuisance edits of its own claimed cells, and
converts unsupported positives into abstentions.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1) A procedural benchmark that emits aerial views with pixel-exact masks,
camera geometry, calibrated degradations, and matched counterfactual
interventions, with source-consensus and resolvability gates and an optically
defined extinction band. (2) A black-box causal evidence protocol that measures
necessity and sufficiency of a model's \emph{own} stated evidence alongside
oracle-mask reliance, under three suppression operators with reported ranking
stability. (3) Result-independent validation instruments---synthetic-insert
positive controls, edit detectability, operator agreement, $K$ sensitivity,
reference baselines, and suppression efficacy---so that a null causal result is
interpretable rather than ambiguous. (4) Per-model acuity thresholds and
selective-risk frontiers that report not how often a model is right but how much
of its output can be trusted at what cost, and CAVE, a training-free
accept-or-abstain wrapper reported as a reliability--coverage--cost frontier.

\section{Related Work}
Broad geospatial VLM evaluations such as GEOBench-VLM and OmniEarth cover
diverse perception, reasoning, grounding, and robustness tasks
\cite{danish2025geobench,fu2026omniearth}. CanyonBench-Trace does not compete on
breadth; it contributes a controlled causal instrument for a narrow deployment
slice.

The closest counterfactual work verifies expert-annotated chest-X-ray regions
through foreground and background editing and evaluates attribution against those
causal regions \cite{xiong2026visual}; that combination of correctness filtering,
foreground deletion, and background-invariance checking is the nearest neighbour
to this work, and we position against it directly rather than around it. Its
companion attribution method requires token log-probabilities, which restricts it
to open-weight systems. Counterfactual edits also expose segmentation
hallucination \cite{li2025hallusegbench}, and whole-image controls and benchmarks
of prior versus visual evidence expose answers that persist without supporting
pixels \cite{zafar2026medical,sun2026prive}. Our distinct object is the model's
own spatial self-report: the protocol intervenes on cells the queried black-box
model names, in addition to exact oracle masks, and needs only repeated queries
and a structured answer, so proprietary and open-weight models are evaluated
identically. That prior work's own ablation, in which bounding-box zero masking
outperformed diffusion inpainting for causal attribution, independently supports
our choice of transparent suppression as the primary operator.

ERASER established comprehensiveness, sufficiency, and perturbation-curve
evaluation for rationales \cite{deyoung2020eraser}; Jacovi and Goldberg separate
faithfulness from plausibility \cite{jacovi2020faithfulness}. Demonstrations of
unfaithful verbal reasoning further motivate intervention rather than accepting
self-explanation at face value \cite{turpin2023unfaithful,lanham2023faithfulness}.
Finally, ICE shows that conclusions can vary sharply across intervention
operators \cite{basu2026ice}, motivating our required O1--O3 agreement and
grid/$K$ sensitivity analyses.
